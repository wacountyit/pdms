Each night PDMS picks up the changes made in Entra since the last run: it adds new people, updates names and departments, and marks people whose account is disabled or deleted as departed (they are signed out). Nobody is reactivated automatically, except people the sync departed for leaving the PDMS group, once they are back in it. The department comes from the {{ attribute }} field of the Entra account; map each value used there to a PDMS department below. A mapping you add, change, or remove applies at once to the people already synced.
